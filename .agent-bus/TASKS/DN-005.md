# DN-005 — Turkish and Arabic review of the Smile Club dentist scripts

Mode: repo
Branch: codex/DN-005

Goal: the dentist scripts (rev. 25 Sep) are now bilingual by branch.
Dr. Tosun Dental Clinic, a Turkish specialty clinic, uses Turkish + English.
Dental Nation Al Wasl and Al Maher Medical Centre use Arabic + English
(confirmed by Fahad). The Turkish and Arabic are Claude's first drafts. Review
them so each reads as natural, correct and warm to a native speaker, stays
accurate to the English, and stays inside the regulatory wording rules. A
native-speaking dentist does the final check; your job is to get the drafts
there first.

Do the Turkish scripts for those two dentists before anything else:
- Dr. Yahya Tosun (ortho): Video 1 Smile Club and Video 2 The DN Scan.

The Turkish strings behind them are COPY.tr, ANGLE.tr.ortho, ANGLE.tr.general,
TITLE.tr, LANE_VIDEO.scan.tr and LANE_VIDEO.glowup.tr.

Scope — the single source of truth:
- lib/smileclub/scripts.ts, which builds every script:
  - COPY[lang]: the WhatsApp group-message frame (hello, per-group opener,
    Smile Club line, call to action, STOP line), the chair invitation, and
    the video frame.
  - ANGLE[lang][specialty]: the specialty reason (chair + why).
  - TITLE.tr / TITLE.ar: job titles on camera.
  - LANE_VIDEO[lane][lang]: the second, campaign video (scan, firstlook,
    glowup, sos, restore).
  - BRANCH_NAME.ar: Arabic branch names.
- To see the rendered output, run `scriptsFor(dentist, lang)` for every
  dentist × branch language (e.g. with `npx tsx`). The page shows the same
  output: Smile Club → Dentist scripts.
- Context: .agent-bus/PROJECT-THREAD.md and lib/smileclub/segments.ts
  (patients segment).

What to check, minimum bar:
1. MEANING: each Turkish and Arabic line says what the English says: no
   added promises, no dropped conditions ("depending on the plan", "eligible
   treatments", "no obligation", "deducted if treatment goes ahead", prices,
   "within 60 minutes").
2. NATURALNESS: reads like a dentist in Dubai writing to or speaking to their
   own patients, not a translation. Turkish uses the formal "siz" form
   throughout. Arabic is Modern Standard, warm and plain, suitable for Gulf
   patients. Video lines must be comfortable to SAY aloud in about 30
   seconds; flag any line that is too long to speak.
3. GROUP MESSAGES: the WhatsApp texts go to a whole group (active /
   inactive / dormant). Nothing may read as if written to one named person.
   Keep "STOP" as the literal opt-out keyword in every language (the CRM
   listens for it).
4. ARABIC SPECIFICS: dentist names are currently in Latin script inside the
   Arabic text. Propose Arabic-script spellings for every Al Wasl and AMC
   dentist, marked "to confirm with the dentist". Flag masculine and
   feminine wording (e.g. TITLE.ar is masculine; imperatives like "ردّ").
   Propose either neutral wording or a per-dentist variant, as data only.
   Check how the "Smile Club" and "The DN …" brand names sit inside RTL
   text.
5. TURKISH SPECIFICS: suffixes on Latin brand and clinic names (e.g.
   "Smile Club’ı", "Dental Nation’ın"), the job titles in TITLE.tr, and
   whether the hello line
   "Merhaba, ben Dr. … — Dr. Tosun Dental Clinic (Dental Nation ailesinin bir parçası)."
   is natural.
6. REGULATORY: Smile Club is a membership, never insurance. None of
   insurance / coverage / covered / claim / premium / policy / insured may
   appear, and no Turkish or Arabic equivalent either (BANNED_TR_AR: sigorta,
   poliçe, teminat, tazminat, تأمين, بوليصة, تغطية, مطالبة). Flag near-misses
   too, e.g. anything implying the membership "pays for" or "covers" treatment.
   No clinical outcome guarantees in the campaign videos (whitening results,
   "seen today").
7. CONSISTENCY: the same term for the same thing everywhere (check-up,
   cleaning, membership, member rates, urgent dental problem) within each
   language.

Deliverables on the branch:

  Add a short section listing the Arabic-script dentist names proposed.
- You MAY apply the text fixes directly in lib/smileclub/scripts.ts. This is

- Do NOT edit the .tsx file or anything else. List those fixes in the review

- `npx tsc --noEmit -p tsconfig.json` must pass on the branch.

Definition of done: the review file exists, every finding cites a constant
path, any edits compile, and the handoff lists the shoot-critical fixes
(Dr. Tosun and Dr. Dilsad Turkish) separately from the rest.

Out of scope: English copy rewrites, lane choices, prices, UI, the database,
prod anything, merging. Your output is a draft for native-speaker sign-off,
not the final approval.
