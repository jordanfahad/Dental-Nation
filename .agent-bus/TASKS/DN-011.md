## How each number was derived (check the logic, not the rows)

- Ad spend by month: sum of daily spend in the Google Ads and Meta insight
  tables (lane_e.google_ads_insights_raw, lane_e.meta_insights_raw). Google:
  May 6,855; Jun 6,954; Jul 6,997; Aug 6,965; Sep 5,393; 1 to 8 Oct 340.
  Meta: Sep 5,954; 1 to 9 Oct 2,145. "Since May" = 41,600 rounded from
  these. Meta has no rows before September (the account was paused).
- New patients at Al Wasl: Practo appointments with status Arrived or
  Completed, one row per file number (mr_no), first visit = earliest such
  appointment, counted by month, Al Wasl centre only, file numbers starting
  "DN" (the dashboard's own rule in lib/status/registry.ts line 261: DN
  prefixes are new patients from the April 2026 Practo rollout; ORN prefixes
  are existing patients from the old EMR). ORN first visits excluded.
- The 188 correction: the same first-visit count without the branch and
  prefix filters gives 35 (Al Wasl) + 153 (Dr Tosun) = 188 in September. Dr
  Tosun's clinic has Practo rows from July only, so its "first visits" are
  mostly existing patients being registered.
- 268 chats, 1 booking, 144 unreached: lib/smileclub/briefingNotes.ts lines
  179, 238 and 261 (268 is Meta's count; 144 of 212 is the desk tracker as at
  5 Oct).
- 3 of 97 tasks done: lane_e.tasks where source = 'smileclub-team'; 97 rows,
  3 with status done, 71 with a due date before 9 Oct and not done.
- Zavis AED 2,174 a month: config/competitors.ts (DN_CRM_MONTHLY), from
  March 2026 invoices.
- Paid media changes: Google spend from 1 Oct is only the Performance Max
  campaign (about AED 30 to 60 a day). Meta Coffee Stains and Smoking Stains
  have no spend after 1 Oct. DN Ortho still had spend on 6, 7 and 8 Oct
  (AED 22, 22, 12), so "stopped today" is a commitment Fahad must execute
  before sending, not a fact yet. Flag this.
