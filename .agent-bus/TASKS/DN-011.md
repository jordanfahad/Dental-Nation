## How each number was derived (check the logic, not the rows)

- Ad spend by month: sum of daily spend in the Google Ads and Meta insight
  tables (lane_e.google_ads_insights_raw, lane_e.meta_insights_raw). Google:
  May 6,855; Jun 6,954; Jul 6,997; Aug 6,965; Sep 5,393; 1 to 8 Oct 340.
  Meta: Sep 5,954; 1 to 9 Oct 2,145. "AED 41,600" is the May to September
  total rounded. Meta has no rows before September (the account was paused).
  October run rate: Google about AED 40 a day, Meta about AED 240 a day
  before DN Ortho stops; "capped at AED 9,000" for October assumes that.
- New patients at Al Wasl: Practo appointments with status Arrived or
  Completed, one row per file number (mr_no), first visit = earliest such
  appointment, counted by month, Al Wasl centre only, file numbers starting
  "DN" (the dashboard's own rule in lib/status/registry.ts line 261: DN
  prefixes are new patients from the April 2026 Practo rollout; ORN prefixes
  are existing patients from the old EMR). ORN first visits excluded.
- Value of a new patient: Practo bills (lane_e.practo_bills_raw, Al Wasl
  centre, bill_status not CANCELLED, net_amount) joined to those new
  patients by file number. Billed within 60 days of the first visit, by
  first-visit month: May 15 patients AED 29,525; Jun 24 / 121,980; Jul 20 /
  56,609; Aug 21 / 28,742 (80 patients, AED 236,856, so about AED 2,960 each,
  rounded to 3,000). Sep 27 / 110,182 (window still open). Note the June
  cohort is far above the others (AED 5,083 each) and August far below (AED
  1,369); check whether the AED 3,000 average is a fair planning figure or
  whether a median would be more honest. The monthly counts in the note
  (15, 24, 20, 22, 26) and in the billing join (15, 24, 20, 21, 27) differ
  by one in Aug and Sep because of a file-number casing difference.
- The 188 correction: the same first-visit count without the branch and
  prefix filters gives 35 (Al Wasl) + 153 (Dr Tosun) = 188 in September. Dr
  Tosun's clinic has Practo rows from July only, so its "first visits" are
  mostly existing patients being registered.
- 268 chats, 1 booking, 144 unreached, 57% aligners or braces, AED 18,500
  case: lib/smileclub/briefingNotes.ts lines 78, 179, 238 and 261 (268 is
  Meta's count; 144 of 212 is the desk tracker as at 5 Oct; 18,500 is a

## What to check, minimum bar
1. HIS THREE QUESTIONS: take each of the owner's three questions in turn and
   say whether the note answers it in the first sentence of its section, or
   buries it. "How many members" must be answered with a number, even if
   the number is zero.
2. ARITHMETIC and DEFINITIONS: do the figures above support every sentence?
   Is "new patient" one definition throughout? Does "20 from AED 9,000,
   about AED 60,000 billed" follow (20 x 3,000)? Is the rule (one patient
   per AED 3,000 of monthly cost) sound, given that billed revenue is not
   margin and that some of those patients would have come anyway? Propose
   the honest version of the rule if not.
3. KPI QUALITY: for each of the three tests and the two-of-three rule, is it
   measurable with data that exists today (the desk source field only
   started 7 Oct; members are counted by code), is the baseline stated, is
   the date realistic, is it within Marketing's control, could it be gamed?
   Is "two of three" the right rule, or should the owner be offered one
   number? Propose tighter wording where it fails.
4. THE "ANALYST" PERCEPTION: the owner thinks Marketing produces reports, not
   money. Does any sentence feed that (reports, dashboards, briefings,
   calendars, analysis)? Flag each one and say whether to cut it.
5. TONE: any line that reads as excuse, blame (especially towards the
   operations team), self-praise, or jargon; is the correction paragraph
   right to include, and is the wording right for an owner who is already
   angry?
6. OWNER READER TEST: the five hardest questions he will ask after reading
   (what the salary bought in the past four months; why the 188 was wrong;
   whether 20 traced patients would have come anyway; what the 144 calls
   cost the desk; what happens on day 31), and whether the note answers
   each or leaves it open.
7. A REVISED VERSION: your own best one-page version, at most 450 words,
   under his three questions, plain English, British spelling, no em dashes,
   numbers unchanged unless the derivation above shows an error.
