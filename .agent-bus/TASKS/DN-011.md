## How each number was derived (check the logic, not the rows)

- Ad spend by month: sum of daily spend in the Google Ads and Meta insight
  tables (lane_e.google_ads_insights_raw, lane_e.meta_insights_raw). Google:
  May 6,855; Jun 6,954; Jul 6,997; Aug 6,965; Sep 5,393; 1 to 8 Oct 340.
  Meta: Sep 5,954; 1 to 9 Oct 2,145. Meta has no rows before September (the
  account was paused). October run rate: Google about AED 40 a day, Meta
  about AED 240 a day before DN Ortho stops; "about AED 9,000" for October
  assumes that run rate continues.
- New patients at Al Wasl: Practo appointments with status Arrived or
  Completed, one row per file number (mr_no), first visit = earliest such
  appointment, counted by month, Al Wasl centre only, file numbers starting
  "DN" (the dashboard's own rule in lib/status/registry.ts line 261: DN
  prefixes are new patients from the April 2026 Practo rollout; ORN prefixes
  are existing patients from the old EMR). ORN first visits excluded.
- Value of a new patient: Practo bills (lane_e.practo_bills_raw, Al Wasl
  centre, bill_status not CANCELLED, net_amount) joined to those new
  patients by file number. Billed within 60 days of the first visit, by
  first-visit month: May 15 patients AED 29,525; Jun 24 / 121,980; Jul 20 /
  56,609; Aug 21 / 28,742 (80 patients, AED 236,856, so about AED 2,960 each,
  rounded to 3,000). Sep 27 / 110,182 (window still open). Billed to date for
  the May to Sep cohorts (107 patients): AED 365,849. Note the June cohort is
  far above the others (AED 5,083 each) and August far below (AED 1,369);
  check whether the AED 3,000 average is a fair planning figure or whether a
  median would be more honest. The monthly counts in the note (15, 24, 20,
  22, 26) and in the billing join (15, 24, 20, 21, 27) differ by one in Aug
  and Sep because of a file-number casing difference; both sum to 107.
- The 188 correction: the same first-visit count without the branch and
  prefix filters gives 35 (Al Wasl) + 153 (Dr Tosun) = 188 in September. Dr
  Tosun's clinic has Practo rows from July only, so its "first visits" are
  mostly existing patients being registered.
- 268 chats, 1 booking, 144 unreached: lib/smileclub/briefingNotes.ts lines
  179, 238 and 261 (268 is Meta's count; 144 of 212 is the desk tracker as at
  5 Oct).
- 3 of 97 tasks done: lane_e.tasks where source = 'smileclub-team'; 97 rows,
  3 with status done, 71 with a due date before 9 Oct and not done.
- Zavis AED 2,174 a month: config/competitors.ts (DN_CRM_MONTHLY), from
  March 2026 invoices.
- Smile Club member value: AED 99 a month (lib/smileclub/scripts.ts line
  168); 40 members = AED 3,960, shown as "AED 4,000".
- Paid media changes: Google spend from 1 Oct is only the Performance Max

  have no spend after 1 Oct. DN Ortho still had spend on 6, 7 and 8 Oct
  (AED 22, 22, 12), so "stopped today" is a commitment Fahad must execute

2. KPI QUALITY: for each of the three KPIs and the decision rule, is it
   measurable with data that exists today (the desk source field only

   baseline stated, is the date realistic, is it within Marketing's control,
   and could it be gamed? Is "two of three" the right rule, or should the
   owner be offered a single number? Propose tighter wording where it fails.
3. OWNER READER TEST: list the five hardest questions the owner will ask
   after reading (what the salary bought in the past four months, why the
   correction was not caught, whether 20 traced patients is new revenue or
   patients who would have come anyway, what happens to the systems if the
   role ends, what the day-31 decision costs him), and whether the note
   answers each or leaves it open.
4. TONE: any line that reads as excuse, blame (especially towards the
   operations team), self-praise, or jargon; is section 4 ("what the role
   does that no one else here does") persuasive or defensive to an owner;
   is the correction paragraph right to include, and is the wording right.
5. MISSING: what a one-page owner note of this kind should carry that this
   one does not, within 450 words.
6. A REVISED VERSION: your own best one-page version, at most 450 words,
