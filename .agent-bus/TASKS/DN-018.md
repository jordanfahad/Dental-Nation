   with tests). Map the raw `clinic` text to one of: `dental-nation`,

   case-insensitive variants. Anything not on the list is `unassigned`. Do
   not guess from partial matches.
   Owner-confirmed locations (Fahad, 13 Oct 2026): Al Wasl is the Dental
   Nation main branch, on Al Badaa St. AMC (Al Maher Centre) is near
   Jumeirah. Dr Tosun Dental Clinic is in Umm Suqeim.
   - `dental-nation`: `Al Wasl`, `AL Wasl`, `Al wasl`, `DN Al Wasl`,
     `Dental Nation - Al Badaa St.`.
   - `dr-tosun`: `Dr. Tosun Dental Clinic Brach`, `Tosun Clinic`.
   - `al-maher`: `AMC BRANCH`.
   - `unassigned` (and listed in the handoff as needing owner confirmation):
     `TDC Al wasl 4 Jumeirah - Branch` (75 rows: it contains both "TDC" and
     "Al wasl", and the owner has not said which branch it is),
     `Al Wasl / Al Maher`, `N/A`, null.
   - Header echoes (`Clinic / Branch`) are `unassigned` and are excluded from
     all counts as `header-echo`. Report that count separately.
   - Note: `lib/performance/core.ts` `parseGrossRows` currently matches
     `tosun|\btdc\b` and `al[ -]?wasl` on the combined branch text, so
     `TDC Al wasl 4 Jumeirah` hits both and becomes `clinic: null`. Switch
     `parseGrossRows` to the new canonicaliser. Its output for the other
     variants must not change, and the gross count must not change for any row
     that canonicalises to a known branch. Test both.

2. **Header echo and blank rows** (same module). A row is a header echo when
   its `Date` value is not a date and equals a header name (`Date`, `Clinic /
   Branch`, `Lead Stage`, `Conversion`, `Conversion - Dr Luvi`, `Inquiry
   Platform`, `Source Type`). Exclude header echoes from every count. Count
   them as `headerEchoRows`. A row with no date is counted as `noDate` and is
   excluded from the day-based windows. It is never assigned to a day.

3. **Future and invalid dates.** A date after today (Dubai) is kept in the
   data but counted as `futureDated` and excluded from the day windows. Do not
   correct it. Do not guess the intended date. Report the count.

4. **Outcome, not booking.** Stop treating `booking_status` as a booked
   signal. Add `readOutcome(raw_row)` that returns the first non-empty,
   non-`N/A`, non-header value from `Conversion - Dr Luvi`, then `Conversion`,
   then `Lead Stage`, plus a count of rows with an outcome recorded. Show
   "Outcome recorded: N of M logged" in place of "booked". Do not classify
   outcomes as booked, showed or lost; that needs the owner's list of values.
   Bookings and shows come from Growth's appointment data, as they do now.

5. **Update DN-017 Part A's reception section** (the same branch, after this
   task merges): the `booked` column described there becomes "Outcome

   `unassigned` shown as its own row. Everything else in DN-017 Part A stands.
