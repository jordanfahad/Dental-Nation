## Review every positive keyword (122 texts)

For each: right pod, right ad group, real patient intent in Dubai, match
type (Exact / Phrase only). Flag and fix:
- Wrong pod or intent overlap between pods (for example `retainer` terms in
  S1, `kids braces` in S1, adult aligner terms in S3). One home per
  intent; add cross-negatives where needed.
- Research or DIY intent that will not book (`how long`, `does it hurt`,
  `at home`, `cost of ... in india`) unless the ad group is meant for it.
- Missing high-intent variants: `near me`, area names (Jumeirah, Al Wasl,
  Umm Suqeim, Al Barsha, Al Safa), `price` / `cost` / `how much`,
  `orthodontist dubai`, `clear braces`, `ceramic braces`, `retainer
  replacement dubai`, `teeth moved after braces`, `child orthodontist
  dubai`, `first orthodontic check age`. Add only if the intent is clear.
- Common misspellings (`invisaline`, `invisilign`, `orthodentist`,
  `orthodentics`, `breces`): keep in the matching ad group.
- Arabic: keep in the separate paused Arabic groups; check each term is
  natural Gulf/MSA usage (`تقويم الأسنان`, `تقويم شفاف`, `تقويم أسنان
  الأطفال`, `مقوم الأسنان`, `تقويم دبي`). Mark `needs native review`.
- Brand terms (`invisalign`): keep, but flag as dependent on the owner's
  Invisalign provider decision; ad copy stays generic.
- Ad groups must stay 5 to 20 keywords.

## Review every negative (192 shared + cross-negatives + 19 held)

- Prove no negative blocks any positive (exact, phrase, word order,
  plurals). Re-run with your own matcher, not DN-021's.
- Find gaps: jobs (`vacancy`, `salary`, `course`, `dha exam`), cheap
  products (`amazon`, `aliexpress`, `kit`, `wax`), other countries and
  emirates (`abu dhabi`, `sharjah`, `ajman`, `india`, `pakistan`,
  `egypt`), other treatments (`implant`, `veneers`, `whitening`,
  `root canal`), animals, `images`, `pdf`, `wikipedia`, `reddit`.
  Plurals and misspellings listed explicitly (negatives do not match close
  variants).
- Remove negatives that block buyers, for example `price` or `cost`
  (keep), or a bare `kids` in S1 that would also block an adult asking for
  a family appointment (use cross-negatives by pod instead).
- The 19 held terms (`free`, `cheap`, `insurance`, `installment`,
  competitor/at-home brands): for each, give a recommendation (block /
  keep / exact-only) with one line of reasoning. Do not decide; the owner
  decides.
