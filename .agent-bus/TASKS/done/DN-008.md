## 1. Row cap bug (do this first, it changes numbers everywhere)
Supabase returns at most 1,000 rows per request unless the reader pages with
`.range()`. practo_appointments_raw already has about 2,200 rows and
practo_bills_raw about 1,400, but lib/growth/channelPerformance.ts,
lib/growth/channelTrace.ts and lib/analytics/demandToDesk.server.ts read them
with a bare `.select()`. The reports therefore see an arbitrary 1,000 rows.
- Add `lib/supabase/selectAll.ts`: a typed helper that pages with
  `.order(<stable key>).range(i, i + 999)` until a short page, with a hard
  ceiling (e.g. 50,000 rows) that throws instead of truncating silently.
- Use it for every unbounded read of a table that can pass 1,000 rows in
  lib/growth, lib/analytics, lib/smileclub, lib/ops and lib/practo (grep for
  `.from(` reads without `.range`/`.limit`/`.eq` on an id). List each changed
  call site in the handoff.
- Unit test with a fake client that serves 2,345 rows in pages.

## 2. One paid-search number (the "23 vs 30" complaint)
The email to Dr Luvi said Google Ads produced about 23 bookings in September.
That used website taps: WhatsApp taps × 0.21 + phone taps × 0.1575 (benchmark
chain 0.75 valid × 0.75 answered × 0.8 patient × 0.35 booked; WhatsApp skips
the answer step). The dashboard's paid-search row and drill-down use only the
call button on the ad itself (google_ads_click_types, 5 taps in September),
so the two can never agree.
- New GA4 daily read (extend lib/sync/adapters/ga4-device.ts or a sibling):
  date × eventName × sessionSourceMedium (and sessionGoogleAdsCampaignName
  where present) for whatsapp_click, phone_click, whatsapp_widget_open,
  generate_lead. Migration 0045_ga4_lead_events_source.sql, table
  lane_e.ga4_lead_events_source (day, event_name, source_medium, campaign,
  event_count, fetched_at), unique (day, event_name, source_medium, campaign).
- phonePath in channelPerformance.ts becomes three measured inputs:
  ad call-button taps, website WhatsApp taps from google / cpc, website phone
  taps from google / cpc. Same benchmark chain, same pool cap and same
  deterministic patient selection as today. Expose each input separately.
- The paid-search drill-down (components/sections/growth/GrowthPlatform.tsx,
  ChannelTraceView) opens with ONE sentence that adds up, e.g.
  "Modelled bookings from Google Ads: 23 = 1 from ad call taps + 16 from
  website WhatsApp taps + 6 from website phone taps. They are drawn from the
  N patients who booked by phone or WhatsApp with no source (listed below)."
  The pool count must never be shown on its own as if it were the channel's
  bookings. Same wording style on the G4 card.
- Put the formula in one shared function used by both the dashboard and the
  email (lib/smileclub/briefingNotes.ts luviAnswersHtml computes it inline
  today), so the two cannot drift again.

## 3. Searches / GBP never 0 or empty
Today a theme shows 0 or blank because it relies on 1 to 3 seed terms. Do what
a media buyer does in Keyword Planner: list every keyword for the condition and
sum the average monthly searches.
- config/demand-themes.ts: give every theme a full seed list, English and
  Arabic, including spelling variants. Example for Gaps & spacing: tooth gap,
  teeth gap, gap teeth, gap in front teeth, gap between teeth, teeth spacing,
  spaces between teeth, diastema, diastema treatment, gap teeth fix, close
  teeth gap, gap teeth braces, gap teeth bonding, gap teeth veneers, plus
  "dubai" and "price"/"cost" variants and Arabic (فراغات الأسنان، فلجة الأسنان،
  تباعد الأسنان، سد الفراغات بين الأسنان). Do the same depth for every theme.
- lib/analytics/demandMarket.ts: per theme, expand the seeds with DataForSEO
  keywords_data/google_ads/keywords_for_keywords (UAE 2784; English and
  Arabic as separate requests), keep only keywords that match the theme
  pattern, then get volumes with google_ads/search_volume. Store the keyword
  list with volumes in the cached snapshot (same 7-day TTL, same domain key).
  Respect the existing cost guard; log request count.
- Searches cell = sum of average monthly searches over the theme's keyword
  list, with "n keywords" under it and a click-to-expand list (keyword,
  searches, CPC, competition). A keyword with no volume counts as 0 but the
  theme total must come from the full list; if a theme still sums to 0, show
  "Under 10 a month (n keywords checked)" instead of a 0 or a dash.
- GBP cell: match gmb_search_keywords against the full theme keyword list and
  pattern (not only the seeds). GBP hides terms under its reporting floor, so
  when nothing matches show "Below Google's reporting floor" rather than 0.

## 4. Replace "Product focus" with learnings and actions
Remove the "Product focus / Modelled screening rule, not a spending
commitment" block and its jargon. Replace it with a section titled
"What we learned and what we do next", one row per theme that has any demand
or any spend, columns:
- What the market wants (searches/month, cost per click, competition in plain
  words: low / medium / high).
- What we ran (Meta spend, chats, cost per chat; which creatives, see 5).
- What happened at the desk (leads, reached, interested, booked, attended).
- What we learned (one plain sentence generated from the numbers, e.g.
  "High demand and cheap clicks, but most leads were not reached: the leak is
  the first call, not the ad.").
- What we do next (one action: scale, keep, fix the desk step, fix the
  creative, pause), Owner (Marketing, Front desk, Dr Luvi, Fahad) and By when.
Above the table, a box "Three decisions for Mr Akbar" picked from the rows
(largest demand not funded, largest leak, best cost per booked patient).
Rules: no em dashes, no AI jargon ("modelled screening rule", "MVM", "MTA"),
every sentence must be traceable to a number on the page. Keep PRODUCT_RULES
thresholds in config, but they choose the action; they are never printed as
a "rule".
