## Patient situations (fixed)

- **S1 Adults, discreet correction**: adults who want straighter teeth
  without obvious braces (clear aligners, ceramic/clear braces, crowding,
  gaps, bite).
- **S2 Relapse after previous treatment**: teeth moved back after braces or
  aligners; lost/broken retainers; retreatment.
- **S3 Parents unsure about timing**: parents asking when/whether their child
  or teen needs orthodontics. Education first. The assessment is NOT a
  treatment commitment; "observe / no treatment needed yet" is a valid,
  respected outcome. No pressure, no urgency language, no discount pressure.
- S4 is education only (no Search campaign).
