## Context

Dr Luvi asked for revised Smile Club video scripts: natural, educational,
patient-focused, friendly, no prices, no sales or pushy tone. The dentists
film in Gulf/Levantine clinics in Dubai and speak in their own dialect on
camera; the written Arabic is the reference for the key points, so it must
be clear Modern Standard Arabic that reads naturally aloud, not a literal
translation of the English. Dr Suzanna will adapt hers to her own dialect.

## What to check, line by line (IDs like `maysoun-3`)

1. Meaning matches the English line (nothing added, nothing lost).
2. Grammar, spelling, hamza and taa marbuta, agreement (feminine titles
   for female dentists: طبيبة / أخصائية), plural "you" (كم) used
   consistently.
3. Natural spoken flow for a 60 to 75 second video: flag anything that
   sounds translated, stiff, or too long to say in one breath, and give a
   smoother version.
4. Tone: friendly and educational. Flag anything that sounds like a sales
   pitch, pressure, urgency or a promise of results.
5. No prices, fees or discounts. No clinical claim stronger than the English.
6. Dental terms are the ones UAE patients use (e.g. تقويم، قوالب شفافة،
   مثبّت، جير، تنظيف احترافي، الأسنان اللبنية). Suggest the more common
   word where one exists.
7. Brand handling: keep "Smile Club" in Latin letters and "Dr. Tosun Dental
   Clinic" in Latin letters; "دنتال نيشن" and "مركز الماهر الطبي" in Arabic.
   Check that a line starting with a Latin word (e.g. "وSmile Club")
   reads correctly right-to-left, and propose a rewording if it would
   display awkwardly.

## Handoff format (`outbox/DN-026.md`)

Front matter `task: DN-026`, `status: complete`, `verdict: ok` or
`verdict: changes`. Then a table with one row per line that needs a change:
`ID | current Arabic | suggested Arabic | why (one line)`. Lines that are
fine are not listed. End with a short overall note (max 5 lines) and
`## Thread`. Note clearly that a native speaker should still give the final
sign-off.
