# DN-007 — "Demand to desk": one-page funnel from ad idea to front desk, by treatment theme
Mode: repo
Branch: codex/DN-007
Goal: Mr Akbar asked (6 Oct) whether the demand ideas we run on Meta match real
market demand in Dubai, and where people are lost between the first ad touch and
the front desk. Build a printable one-page view that follows each treatment
theme from market demand → our ad idea → enquiry → front desk → booked →
attended, and flags the biggest leak per theme. Code only: no data in the repo.
Context:
- Themes (classify by regex over names/keywords/text; keep the map in one file,
  e.g. config/demand-themes.ts): Gaps & spacing; Protruding / crooked teeth
  (aligners, braces, ortho); Stains & whitening (coffee, smoking); Bad breath &
  hygiene (cleaning, check-up); Gummy smile; Emergency / pain; Implants &
  missing teeth; Other.
- Stage sources (Supabase schema lane_e, read with the existing server client;
  see lib/growth/channelPerformance.ts and lib/ops/metaLeadsDigest.ts for
  patterns):
  1. Market demand: gmb_search_keywords (month, keyword, impressions) and the
     latest competitor_snapshots row for dentalnation.com (DataForSEO ranked
     keywords with search volume, Dubai). Sum monthly searches per theme.
  2. Our ideation (Meta): meta_ad_insights_raw (campaign_name, ad_name, spend,
     data.actions). Per theme: spend, people who started a chat
     (messaging_conversation_started_7d), net (messaging_user_depth_2_message_send).
     Reuse metaPeople() logic from lib/ops/metaLeadsDigest.ts; never sum events.
  3. Enquiry at the desk: lane_e.leads + raw_lead_tracker (columns
     "Service / Inquiry Type", "Notes / Remarks", "New Update", follow-up
     columns, "Conversion"/"Conversion - Dr Luvi"). Per theme: leads logged,
     reached (a reply or call recorded), no answer, stated barriers (language,
     price, not interested, international number), booked.
  4. Clinic: practo_appointments_raw (complaint, department_name, package_name,
     appointment_status, mr_no) for NEW patient files (mr_no starting "DN") in
     the window: booked, arrived/completed, cancelled/no-show per theme.
- Output: lib/analytics/demandToDesk.ts (pure functions: classify, aggregate,
  leak = largest relative drop between consecutive stages) and a server
  component rendered under the Marketing tab (sub-tab "Demand to desk") with a
  date range (default last full month). One A4-landscape page when printed:
  a theme × stage table with counts and stage-to-stage %, the leak highlighted,
  a "demand vs ideation" column (share of market searches vs share of Meta
  spend per theme), and 3 auto-generated one-line findings.
- Added 6 Oct (Fahad, after the meeting with Mr Akbar). Three more blocks on
  the same page:
  a. Demand on Meta itself, per theme and per week: impressions, reach,
     frequency, CPM, cost per chat started and chat rate (chats / reach), from
     meta_ad_insights_raw (+ data json). Stable or falling cost per chat while
     spend rises = demand is there; rising cost with flat chats = saturated.
     Compare with targets where config/kpi-benchmarks.ts or the Smile Club plan
     has one (cost per chat, cost per net lead, booking rate); show "no target
     set" otherwise.
  b. Lead quality trend (the case for patience and budget): weekly cohorts
     since the Meta launch (16 Sep): chats → interested → booked → converted /
     attended. "Interested", "booked", "converted" are classified from the
     tracker text columns ("Conversion", "Conversion - Dr Luvi", "Lead Stage",
     "New Update", "Notes / Remarks", follow-up columns) with a documented
     keyword list (e.g. "new patient converted", "booked", "appointment",
     "interested", "will visit") plus Practo attendance for new files; show
     the first cohort vs the latest cohort side by side.
  Outcome source (confirmed by Fahad 6 Oct): tab "DN Campaign Leads  ❤",
     columns P–W: "1st Follow up", "2nd Follow-up", "3rd Follow-up",
     "New Update", "Assigned To", "Notes / Remarks", "Conversion - Dr Luvi",
     "Revenue". A lead's current status = the latest non-empty of 3rd → 2nd →
     1st follow-up / New Update. Observed values to classify: "called NA /
     sent WhatsApp" (no answer), "interested"/"intersted", "international
     number", "no English", "not interested", "expensive / not interested",
     "out of budget", "booked", "arrived", "rescheduled", "located in Ajman" /
     "looking for clinic in Jabal Ali" (out of area). Typos are common; match
     loosely and keep the keyword list in config.
  c. Follow-up discipline at the desk (is each enquiry treated as a sales
     call?): per week, share of tracker leads with a 1st / 2nd / 3rd
     follow-up recorded, share with a call logged (lead_call_log), share with
     a next action or date, and leads with no follow-up at all. Counts only.
- Added 6 Oct (2): PRODUCT FOCUS block, "which condition should we sell, given
  Dubai demand, competition and our own results". One row per theme:
  1. Market demand, UAE (DataForSEO Labs keyword_overview, location 2784,
     languages en and ar): monthly searches, CPC and competition index for a
     keyword set per theme, e.g. gaps: "tooth gap", "gap teeth treatment",
     "diastema treatment dubai", "فراغات الأسنان"; aligners/braces: "invisalign
     dubai", "clear aligners dubai", "braces price dubai", "تقويم الأسنان";
     whitening: "teeth whitening dubai", "teeth whitening price", "تبييض
     الأسنان"; cleaning/bad breath: "teeth cleaning dubai", "scaling and
     polishing", "bad breath treatment", "تنظيف الأسنان"; gummy smile: "gummy
     smile treatment dubai"; implants: "dental implants dubai", "implant cost
     dubai", "زراعة الأسنان"; veneers: "veneers dubai", "hollywood smile
     dubai", "ابتسامة هوليود"; emergency: "emergency dentist dubai". Keep the
     sets in config/demand-themes.ts. Reuse the post()/labs() helpers and
     credentials pattern in lib/analytics/competitor.ts. Cache the result as a
     row in lane_e.competitor_snapshots with domain 'market:uae-dental'
     (existing table, no migration), refreshed weekly from the sync like the
     competitor snapshots; one call per language, cost well under $1.
  2. Competition: CPC and competition index from (1), plus the number of
     Meta advertisers is optional (only if the Ad Library is reachable; else
     omit, never zero).
  3. Our evidence: Meta cost per chat, chat → interested rate (tracker),
     interested → booked; first-treatment value per theme from a config table
     (aligners/braces 18,500; gummy smile 5,000; whitening 1,699; cleaning /
     first look 799; implants and veneers: "to confirm"), all labelled.
  4. Launch history: every Meta campaign/ad set with first and last active
     date and spend, grouped by theme, so the page shows what we launched with
     and how we narrowed it down (paused = last active date in the past).
  5. A recommendation per theme from a transparent rule: Focus (high demand,
     our cost per interested lead below the theme's break-even, value high),
     Test (demand present, evidence thin), Hold (low demand or competition so
     high our cost per lead is above break-even). Show the rule on the page.
- Honesty rules (PROJECT-THREAD): label modelled vs measured; counts only;
  never render patient names or phone numbers on this page.
