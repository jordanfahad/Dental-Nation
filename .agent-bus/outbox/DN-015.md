## Decisions and data notes

- Reporting ends on yesterday, the last complete Dubai day. The first valid non-future log date starts one shared 30-day plan; branch filtering does not reset it. Future/post-plan rows stay visible but do not affect progress. Entered plan-day disagreements are counted, never silently corrected. On the first of a month, the completed reporting month is yesterday's month; email period dates make that explicit.
- Completion = Done / all valid logged activities. It is labelled logged-activity completion because this task does not import the full plan's task inventory. Partial does not count as Done. Coverage = elapsed plan dates with Done or Partial activity / elapsed days, capped at day 30. Missing dates are listed per branch.
- On track requires coverage >=80% and daily call rate no more than 10% below the pre-plan 28-day baseline. Off track means coverage <50% or calls down >25%. All other cases are Watch. Missing or zero call baselines cannot earn green. Comparisons normalise 7 versus 28 days by daily rate.
- Calls/directions/clicks/views require every day in the requested window, including explicit zero values. Missing days show Unavailable. Reviews use currently active rows and original Dubai creation dates, so removals can restate history. The displayed period rating averages that period's new reviews. A review-sync observation after the window is required to report zero new reviews; profiles without any stored reviews remain unavailable.
- Gross = dated raw tracker rows, including duplicates. Unrecognised dates are counted as excluded, and unambiguous branch labels/source tabs are required for a clinic gross count. Unknown/ambiguous branches remain in Group only. Net uses the existing Growth cross-source phone deduplication, including its individual counting of phoneless rows. Its shared acquisition surfaces remain under Al Wasl, explicitly labelled as not a verified branch split; Dr Tosun/Al Maher net counts are unavailable. No invented clinic allocation.
- Existing Growth taxonomy rolls into the five requested groups: affiliates/influencers join Paid digital; institutional partnerships join Referral. Phone-path estimates are excluded. Non-cancelled billing includes an explicit unattributed row so group revenue/patients reconcile. Group patient files are deduped across clinics; clinic totals are not summed to manufacture a group distinct count.
- Al Wasl new patients reuse `computeNewPatientAcquisition`; existing means other measurable billed files in that reporting window. Transferred Dr Tosun files remain separately unclassified. The mandatory Dr Tosun migration and Al Maher no-feed caveats appear in both daily and MTD tables.
