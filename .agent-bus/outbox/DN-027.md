## What

The weekly UAE keyword cache paid for every related-terms request and then
threw everything away once more than 10,000 terms came back (0 keywords
stored on 7 and 14 Oct, USD 1.53 and 1.44). Now:

- Seeds are always stored.
- Related terms are capped at 150 per theme and 3,000 per language,
  highest search volume first, ties by keyword text. Caps live in
  `MARKET_BUDGET` (`maxRelatedPerTheme`, `maxRelatedPerLanguage`); the old
  `maxKeywords` throw is gone.
- Related terms keep the volume, CPC and competition that
  `keywords_for_keywords` already returns, so only seeds (or terms without
  data) go to the paid `search_volume` request.
- The cap is recorded in a new optional `notes` field, not `errors`, so the
  Demand to desk report stays usable and shows the note as a data gap.
  `parseMarketSnapshot` accepts `notes`; version stays 2.
- Budget guard, TTL, location 2784, themes and seeds unchanged.

## Files

- `lib/analytics/demandMarket.ts`: capped, volume-ranked selection; reuse expansion data; `notes`.
- `lib/analytics/demandToDesk.ts`: `notes` on `MarketSnapshot`; shown in report gaps.
