## Unavailable cells and reasons

- All 2025 patient acquisition, value and retention: earlier patient-level data was never loaded; new-patient file history begins in April 2026. Al Maher has no patient/channel feed at all.
- Months before a branch's first bill, wholly after its loaded coverage, or an absent/failed/invalid bill feed: no observed patient history for that window. Partially covered periods show recorded counts with their dates and the known gap, not full-year claims.
- Dr Tosun new patients, attributed new patients, early LTV and separated returning counts: migrated numbering cannot distinguish new from returning. Distinct billed files and repeat-month files remain available where observed.
- Al Maher spend: no spend recorded. Al Wasl months before the first ad-account row and wholly after the latest row: no observed account spend for that period. Dr Tosun's ad-account spend is explicitly 0 under the Al Wasl-only account attribution rule.
- Failed/invalid ad feeds: combined ad-account spend unavailable. Per-platform first/latest dates accompany available totals; later unsynced days are not estimated.
- Manual spend with no matching branch/period rows, a failed/invalid sheet or an unsplittable campaign-period total: no recorded daily amount available for that cell. Manual spend is never added to the Executive account total.
- Failed attribution evidence: channel groups and detail unavailable rather than silently defaulting every patient. With readable evidence but no matching trace, Direct / Walk-in is the labelled default and still reconciles to all new patients.
- Blended cost with unavailable spend/counts, no new patients or no positive ad spend: no computable Executive-equivalent denominator/numerator.
- Early cohort value before May 2026, no eligible cohort, or failed bills: insufficient cohort history. Fixed 30/90-day values require that much observed history for the youngest member. Month 1/2/3 retention requires the full target calendar month within loaded coverage.
- Revenue context with no matching branch/month in a source, an unavailable source, or only an undated/inconsistent-date amount: cannot allocate to the requested period. Missing months are not estimated. Legacy collected, legacy billed, Zoho billed and Practo bills never sum together.
- No eligible cohorts: history is unavailable, not displayed as zero months.

## Caveats

- Executive `marketingSpend` falls back to its manual tracker if a window has zero live ad rows. DN-012 intentionally leaves pre-account periods unavailable and keeps the Dr Tosun manual campaign separate. Covered account windows use the same live tables and sums; the fallback is disclosed in the page. Executive definitions were not changed.
- Patient coverage is conservatively capped at each branch's latest non-cancelled bill, not a claimed full sync watermark. This can defer a maturity result on a day with no bills. Historic gaps can shift a first observed bill and understate value or returns.
- Attribution retains Growth's all-time evidence, stable appointment-key tie order and existing null-date handling. Estimated phone-path bookings are excluded. The reported 7 October reception-recording start is qualified; the new source field is not consumed by the existing synced attribution path.
- `selectAll` retains its existing 50,000-row safety ceiling; a failed page or exceeded ceiling makes the affected source unavailable, never a partial total.
- No live authenticated browser session was available for end-to-end UI verification. Actual route permission execution, ordinary server rendering, read-only database aggregates and workbook round-trips were checked separately. No requested code feature remains unbuilt.
