Dense A4 typography was checked at the tested page size; other printer engines/scaling were not tested.
- Market demand is UAE-wide, based on the configured keyword set. It is not Dubai-only, a count of unique people, or all dental demand. GBP impressions/threshold ceilings and dentalnation.com's limited ranked-keyword sample remain separately labelled; they are not added to the market search estimate. Provider CPC stays in USD; competition is converted from 0–1 to 0–100, as documented by [DataForSEO](https://docs.dataforseo.com/v3/dataforseo_labs-google-keyword_overview-live/).
- Ad/day chat/net/reach totals can repeat people across ads and dates. Weekly frequency/chat-rate values therefore remain directional. Shared `metaPeople` prevents summing overlapping action families within each row.
- Cross-source stages are unlinked populations. Their ratios are marked directional; the leak calculation highlights only consecutive comparable stages. Clinic counts are distinct DN-prefixed files within each theme, across all channels; they are not verified Meta conversions or proof of a file's creation date.
- Tracker current status uses the declared 3rd → 2nd → 1st → New Update precedence. Positive later milestones imply earlier milestones; negative phrases are handled per stage. An unanswered call is an attempt, not proof the lead was reached. Text matching remains a heuristic rather than an audited event history.
- Cohorts show current tracker outcomes grouped by acquisition week, not historical outcomes as of the range end. Partial weeks and unequal cohort maturity are labelled. The launch date and first-treatment values are task-provided assumptions.
- Bronze tracker rows currently lack source-tab provenance. Future syncs retain it without a migration; existing rows use channel scoping with a visible coverage caveat until resynced. The canonical lead count is for reconciliation, never added to the tracker.
- The call log's references belong to widget leads. Only explicit matching tracker references can establish call coverage; missing links remain unknown. No phone-based linkage or patient data is rendered.
- Financial contribution margins are not supplied. Break-even remains unknown and the rule cannot emit Focus without those inputs. Generic CPL and Smile Club membership targets are not silently repurposed as cost-per-chat/net targets. The applicable booking benchmark is labelled as a planning range.
- Ad-set IDs, reach and frequency arrive only after a future sync. Activity recency is an inference, not a verified pause status. Optional Ad Library advertiser counts are omitted.
- Each source is paginated in 1,000-row pages with stable ordering; over 100,000 rows or a failed page withholds that source's totals. Weekly history is capped at 260 weeks, with a visible coverage note. [Supabase's pagination documentation](https://supabase.com/docs/reference/javascript/range) confirms inclusive range bounds and the need for ordering.
- Cache refresh is wired as code only. It uses the existing DataForSEO credential pattern and existing snapshot table, makes one request per language without paid retries, and caches missing/error evidence explicitly. No migrations or new tables were added. Review is needed before this branch is integrated and its future sync behavior runs.
- The dispatcher model/reasoning pin is not exposed to this process and was not independently verified. No mismatch was identified.
- Existing unrelated untracked agent-bus files were left untouched. Commit hooks and signing were disabled for local commits. No history was rewritten, and nothing was pushed or deployed.
