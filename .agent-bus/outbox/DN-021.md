- The task says four shared lists but names five. All five named lists are supplied. Its tracker wording says 11 rows plus the launch batch, but names seven core rows plus three landing pages plus the batch. All 11 named deliverables are included; no unnamed twelfth row was invented.
- All 19 Review=yes terms are withheld from import and the Shared Library files, including free/cheap variants, insurance/instalments and supplied competitor/at-home brands. They remain visible in the workbook. Ambiguous standalone negatives use Exact to preserve useful longer queries. Matching checks cover literal keyword text, not every future positive close variant or search query. [Google negative keyword rules](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2453972?hl=en).
- Three requested EN campaign drafts retain Paused Arabic review groups. Any Arabic launch requires native review and moving that inventory to AR-named campaigns using SH-03. Generic copy is used for the Invisalign group; provider/service status remains unresolved. Neither Arabic approval nor provider status is asserted.
- S3 says assessment is not a commitment to treatment. Description 1 is pinned to position 1 in each S3 RSA, including Arabic; all other S3 descriptions repeat the notice. No headline pins were needed. Observe / no treatment needed yet is a valid clinical outcome, excluded from sales pressure and sales leakage. Clinical values are provisional pending Dr Hasna's S2-09 codebook.

The proposed move to Maximize conversions requires 15 unique CRM-qualified enquiries per campaign in a rolling 30 days and review of the bidding goal; this is a task assumption, not a Google minimum. Booked and attended remain later secondary offline imports.
- UAE is absent from Google's published forwarding-number availability list. Zavis/Fahad must prove a supported 60-second call-duration and attribution route or revise the measurement plan before launch. A call click cannot stand in for a measured call. Browser Tag Assistant evidence alone cannot establish telephony duration or offline CRM qualification. [Google call reporting](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2454052?hl=en).

The current `performanceChannel()` does not automatically consume these UTM fields, so SH-03 includes explicit legacy tracker mappings for Zavis to implement. No code was changed. A generic phone route does not prove an acquisition source.
- Original attribution is write-once, including unknown values. Latest attribution replaces the whole tuple for each genuine newer enquiry; retries and stale arrivals do not overwrite it. The 14 synthetic cases passed a local reference model only. Same-phone enquiry dedupe does not authorise merging separate parent/child clinical records. No real personal data, contact numbers, credentials or live records were used.
- All seeded funnel rates and historical CPC are labelled assumptions. The target stays blank. The budget split is editable at 50% / 25% / 25%; no spend was set. No search volumes were invented. No paid platform calls were made.
- Files in out/ were delivered once after staging QA. CSVs are static; later workbook edits require reconciliation and another validation before import. No changes to Google Ads, Zavis, CRM, website, production database, email or any live account were made. No push or deployment was run.
